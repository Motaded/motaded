What is the Entrepreneur License?

The Entrepreneur License is a special category of MISA investment registration designed for entrepreneurs and startups looking to establish innovative, distinguished, or emerging technology companies with capacity for expansion and growth in the Saudi market.

Unlike standard foreign investment licenses, the Entrepreneur License requires neither audited financial statements nor a parent company commercial registration. Instead, it requires a Support Letter from an approved supporting entity — a recognized Saudi university or licensed business incubator.

▸  The activity number must be included in the Support Letter
