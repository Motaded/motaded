Reschedule Appointment

  • You can reschedule an appointment only up to the day prior to the appointment date, post which this option will be restricted.
  • If you have an appointment within the next 3 days and you wish to reschedule the appointment, you will not be able to book a normal appointment for the next 7 days. You can book a Premium Lounge appointment only.

Appointment cancellation

  • You can cancel an appointment only up to the day prior to the appointment date, post which this option will be restricted.
  • If you have an appointment within the next 3 days and you wish to cancel the appointment, you will not be able to book a normal appointment for the next 7 days. You can book a Premium Lounge appointment only.

No Show for a previous appointment

If you had an appointment previously but did not visit the Visa Service centre or did not submit your application at the Visa Services Center on the appointment date due to any reason, you will not be able to book a new Normal appointment for the next 7 days. You can book a new Premium Lounge appointment only.

Number of changes in an appointment

If you have rescheduled and/ or cancelled your appointment more than 5 times, you will not be able to book a new Normal appointment for the next 14 days. You can book a new Premium Lounge appointment only.
